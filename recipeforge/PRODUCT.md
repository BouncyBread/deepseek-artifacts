# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home cooks in a shared household, phone-first while cooking. One hand on the phone, one on the spatula. They browse at the counter, glance mid-cook, read from arm's length. Speed and legibility matter.

## Product Purpose

Generate and browse AI-crafted recipes with rich, cuisine-specific visual identity. Each recipe should feel like a page torn from a well-loved cookbook — personal, textured, worth returning to.

## Brand Personality

Warm, handcrafted, intimate. The app should feel like a friend's handwritten recipe card, not a database query. Imperfect warmth over sterile precision. Generous whitespace, rich colors, tactile details.

## Anti-references

- Sterile SaaS dashboards (Stripe, Vercel admin panels)
- Cold clinical interfaces (healthcare portals, banking apps)
- Generic shadcn defaults with no point of view
- Material Design 3 spec-compliant monotony
- Anything you'd describe as "clean," "minimal," or "professional" without qualification

## Design Principles

- **Paper, not screen.** Every surface should evoke physical cookbook materials — paper textures, ink colors, handwritten warmth. The medium is digital, the mood is analog.
- **Each cuisine sings differently.** Thai feels different from French. The per-recipe theming system is the emotional core; amplify it.
- **One-handed, one-glance.** Cooking context drives every layout decision. Type is big. Tap targets are generous. Information hierarchy is immediate.
- **Generosity over restraint.** This is not a minimalist app. Color, texture, illustration, and motion are welcome. Abundance feels like a full kitchen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Legible at arm's length (cooking-distance reading)
- Generous tap targets for wet or flour-dusted fingers
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- WCAG AA for critical flows
